Signing in is optional

Every game on dreamteamhub.ca can be played as a guest. Signing in with Google only matters for leaderboards.

What we store when you sign in

  • A Google account ID. Google gives us a random number that identifies your account to us.
  • Your email address, from Google. Only the site admin can see it, to help with your account or donations. It's never shown to other players. We don't receive your real name or photo.
  • Your display name, like "LuckyOtter". It starts random and you can change it on your profile page. Other players can see it.
  • A login cookie called dth_session, set on dreamteamhub.ca so every game knows you're signed in. It lasts 60 days or until you sign out. We only keep a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.
  • Game results (wins, losses and rankings), linked to your account.
  • Donations and cosmetics. If you support us, the amount and any cosmetic items you've unlocked are recorded on your account.

We don't sell or share this information. It's stored with Cloudflare, which runs our account service.

Delete your account

This permanently deletes your account, email, display name, results, cosmetics and every login session. You can sign in again later, but you'll start over as a new player.
